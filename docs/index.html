IG Cleaning

Clean up your Instagram, at a human pace.

A free Mac app that finds who doesn't follow you back — or who you never talk to — protects influencers and friends, and unfollows the rest slowly enough that Instagram doesn't mind.

⬇ Download for Mac GitHub

Free & open source · macOS 11+ · Apple Silicon and Intel · 18 MB

What it does

Analyzes your account

Everyone you follow, who follows you back and when you last messaged them.

Protects who matters

Verified accounts and anyone over 10,000 followers are shielded. A ★ whitelist keeps friends safe.

Select like in Finder

Click, ⇧-click for a range, ⇧+arrows to extend, ⌘A for all.

Human pace

One every 20–40 s, max 60/hour and 150/day. At the first warning it stops for 24 h.

A receipt

Photo, name and @username of everyone removed, with a Follow again button.

Private

You log in on instagram.com itself. The app never sees your password; data stays on your Mac.

Install in a minute

  1. Open IG-Cleaning.dmg and drag IG Cleaning into Applications.
  2. The first time, macOS says “Apple could not verify ‘IG Cleaning’ is free of malware…”. That's normal for indie apps not notarized by Apple. Click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Only once.
  3. Log in on the Instagram window and press Analyze my account. Pick who to remove and press Unfollow.
